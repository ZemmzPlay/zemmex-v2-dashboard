# 7. Open questions and placeholders

Nothing in this list has been confirmed by the client. Treat every item as invented
until someone says otherwise, and do not put any of it in front of a customer as fact.

## Invented content

- **All people.** Faculty, speakers, artists, judges, organisers, admins and every
  registrant are fictional. The medical event originally used real doctors' names from
  the client's 2020 deck; they were replaced deliberately. Do not restore them.
- **All organisations.** Event names, studios, companies, venues, sponsors and
  partners. Some real venues appear as plausible locations (The Regency Kuwait, Dubai
  Design District, Manarat Al Saadiyat) with fictional events at them.
- **Client logos on the marketing site.** The names under the hero came from the
  client's earlier design. Confirm they are customers of zemmz Live and that they
  agree to be named.
- **Statistics.** 1.2M scans, 2,400 events, 6-second check-in, attendee counts,
  revenue figures and the funding figure on the summit site.
- **The testimonial quote** on the marketing site.
- **Accreditation details.** The CME activity number `002398/CAR1/Sep26` is made up.
  KIMS is real and is the standard the client's deck referenced, but the wording on
  the certificate must be checked against what KIMS actually requires.

## Pricing

See `docs/05-pricing.md`. The model is researched but not approved. Confirm the card
processing rate, the AED 25 cap, VAT presentation and the trial shape.

## Product questions

- **Arabic on Live event sites.** The marketing site and the Play sites promise
  Arabic; the four Live example sites are English only. Either build Arabic into them
  or soften the claim.
- **On-site support** is offered on the government and enterprise plan. Can the client
  actually staff that?
- **SSO, audit exports and a customer's own domain** are listed as enterprise
  features. None are designed.
- **Data residency.** Government and hospital buyers in the GCC frequently require
  data to stay in country. Not addressed anywhere.
- **Who owns attendee data** — worth stating explicitly, since it is a known complaint
  about the marketplace platforms zemmz competes with.
- **Refunds and cancellations** for paid tickets are not designed.
- **Payment provider.** The prototypes show a generic card and Apple Pay checkout.
  A regional provider will be needed.

## Prototype limitations to explain when demoing

- Each prototype is independent: a registration on an event site does not reach the
  dashboard.
- Everything resets on reload.
- Cross-links between prototypes open the medical example first; the viewer then picks
  another event from the switcher.
- The wordmark in the footer is a trace of a PNG, not the client's original vector.
