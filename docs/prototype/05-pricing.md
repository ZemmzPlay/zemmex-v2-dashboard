# 5. Pricing

Prices in the prototypes are a **proposal**, not a decision the client has signed off.
They replaced earlier placeholder numbers in US dollars that had no research behind
them. The model below is the "hybrid" option the client chose.

## 5.1 zemmz Live

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Single event | AED 7,500 per event | One event, up to 1,000 attendees |
| Season | AED 29,000 per year | Up to six events a year, SMS, priority support on event days |
| Government and enterprise | From AED 75,000 per year | Unlimited events, own domain, SSO, on-site support |

**Ticket fee.** Free registration costs nothing per attendee, however many attend.
Paid tickets add **2.5% plus AED 2 per ticket, capped at AED 25**. The organiser can
absorb it or pass it to the buyer at checkout. Card processing is separate and charged
by the payment provider.

## 5.2 zemmz Play

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Club | AED 199 | AED 159/mo | 1 website, up to 2,000 players |
| Season | AED 799 | AED 639/mo | 3 websites, 100,000 players, custom domain, season rankings |
| Publishers and federations | Custom | Custom | Unlimited, SSO, SLA |

Same ticket fee applies to paid entry fees.

## 5.3 The market these numbers sit in

Researched September 2026.

| Platform | What it charges |
|---|---|
| Eventbrite | 3.7% + $1.79 per paid ticket, plus 2.9% processing, **no cap**; about 13.8% of a $25 ticket, 10.2% of a $50 one. Free events free. |
| RegFox | $0.99 + 1% per paid registrant, **capped at $4.99**, no licence fee |
| Whova | Quote only; reported around 3% + $0.99 per paid ticket |
| Swoogo | $7,800–$24,000 a year by number of users, unlimited events and registrations; $11,800 commonly cited |
| Cvent | No published price; median about $19,550 a year, range $5,400–$94,642, plus $7–12 per registrant for the app and check-in, plus $5,000–$50,000 implementation |
| Dubai vendors | About AED 10,000 for small events, AED 25,000–50,000 for large ones |
| Toornament (Play's market) | Free to 128 players; Pro €49–79/mo; white-label site €199/mo; circuits €799/mo; one-off €19 per tournament |

## 5.4 Why the model is shaped this way

- **A cap on the ticket fee.** Uncapped percentages are the standard complaint about
  Eventbrite, and they make the platform unusable for premium tickets: an AED 750 VIP
  ticket would otherwise carry AED 21 of fee. RegFox's cap is the model copied here.
- **Free events pay nothing per head.** Medical congresses and government forums are
  usually free to attend. Charging per registration would price zemmz out of exactly
  the accounts where it is strongest.
- **A fixed annual number for government.** Public bodies budget and tender against a
  fixed figure; revenue share is hard to put in a procurement document.
- **Per-event pricing for societies.** A medical society running one congress a year
  should not buy an annual licence.
- **AED, not USD.** Every buyer is in the Gulf, and the example events already price in
  AED and KWD.
- **Play sells the website, not the brackets.** Free bracket tools are everywhere, so
  the paid tiers start where a branded site and a season circuit begin, which is also
  where Toornament charges.

## 5.5 Decisions still open

- Card processing is typically 2.5–3% in the region; confirm the real rate before the
  2.5% platform fee is quoted publicly, since buyers will add the two together.
- Is AED 25 the right cap? It costs money on premium concert tickets and is the single
  biggest lever on promoter revenue.
- Whether the trial should be time-based or the current "first 50 attendees free".
- VAT presentation: prices are shown excluding VAT.
